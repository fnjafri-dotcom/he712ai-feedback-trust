Trust Lab

Five short pieces of learner work, each with feedback from an AI tool. For each one, decide what you'd do with the feedback. There's no answer key. Reasonable educators will disagree, and that disagreement is what we'll discuss.

How it works. Choose what you'd do with the feedback and how sure you are. Then you'll see a case for using it, a case for caution, and a question to think about. At the end you'll see your own pattern, to bring to your partner discussion.

All cases are fictional and written for teaching. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
